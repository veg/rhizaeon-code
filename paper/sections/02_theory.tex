\section{Geometric Foundations and Continuous Trajectory Theory}
\label{sec:theory}

\subsection{Sequence Metric Space and Buneman Coordinates}
\label{subsec:metric_space}

We ground the method in an authentic viral benchmark: the HIV-1 circulating recombinant forms CRF07\_BC and CRF08\_BC \cite{su2000characterization}.
Both lineages emerged from independent recombination events between Subtype B and Subtype C in China.
They share parental sources across the 3,120-nucleotide \textit{pol} gene.
Real chimeras test theory.
Our alignment comprises 22 sequences: eight Subtype B references, eight Subtype C references, three CRF07\_BC isolates, and three CRF08\_BC isolates.
From these sequences, we compute the global pairwise evolutionary distance matrix $\mathbf{D} \in \mathbb{R}^{22 \times 22}$ under the Tamura-Nei substitution model with pairwise deletion of gap characters, capturing nucleotide transition and transversion rate biases across the retroviral genome \cite{tamura1993estimation}.
We center the squared distances using the projection operator $\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{I}_N - \frac{1}{N} \mathbf{1}_N \mathbf{1}_N^T$, forming the inner-product Gram matrix $\mathbf{B} = -\frac{1}{2} \mathbf{H} \mathbf{D}^{(2)} \mathbf{H}$ \cite{gower1966some}.
Spectral decomposition of $\mathbf{B}$ yields non-negative eigenvalues $\mathbf{\Lambda}_k$ and eigenvectors $\mathbf{V}_k$, projecting each sequence $i$ into a continuous coordinate $\mathbf{x}_i = \mathbf{V}_{k, i} \mathbf{\Lambda}_k^{1/2} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ \cite{buneman1974note}.
The primary axis cleanly separates Subtype B from Subtype C.
Canonical metric coordinates are computed once for the entire alignment.
An invariant coordinate basis eliminates Procrustes alignment overhead and establishes a fixed reference frame for continuous trajectory tracking.

To track sequence affinity along the chromosome, each genome requires an instantaneous coordinate at every position $u \in [1, L]$.
Recalculating multidimensional scaling across sliding windows would distort the geometric space and require expensive Procrustes rotations.
Instead, we project local distance vectors into the fixed coordinate basis using Gower's analytical out-of-sample mapping \cite{gower1968adding}.
At coordinate $u$, sequence $i$ presents an instantaneous distance vector $\mathbf{d}_i(u) \in \mathbb{R}^N$ to the $N$ reference genomes, projecting to $\mathbf{z}_i(u) = \frac{1}{2} \mathbf{\Lambda}_k^{-1/2} \mathbf{V}_k^T (\bar{\mathbf{d}}^2 - \mathbf{d}_i^2(u))$ where $\bar{\mathbf{d}}^2 = \frac{1}{N} \mathbf{D}^{(2)} \mathbf{1}_N$ represents the mean squared reference distance.
When applied to our HIV-1 benchmark, the resulting coordinate trajectories reveal the physical dynamics of genetic exchange.
Non-recombinant Subtype B and Subtype C genomes remain stationary within their respective corridors across the entire gene.
Wobble never crosses clades.
Point mutations generate harmless local flutter around parental medoids, but unreticulated lineages never breach the inter-subtype divide.
In sharp contrast, the recombinant CRF07\_BC trajectory tracks within Subtype C territory through Protease, abruptly vaults across the zero-axis into Subtype B space in Reverse Transcriptase, and plunges back into Subtype C.
CRF08\_BC shares the identical parental gene pool but enters the Subtype B cassette approximately 130 nucleotides upstream.
In two-dimensional metric space, the two chimeras trace distinct orbital loops, resolving competing recombinant structures in linear time without triplet enumeration.
Manifolds preserve chimeras.

\subsection{Rotary Phase Embeddings and Markov Substitution Priors}
\label{subsec:rotary_prior}

Standard transformer architectures encode sequential position along one-dimensional strings using Rotary Position Embeddings (Tree-RoPE), but biological sequences diverge across multidimensional evolutionary space.
Linear string offsets cannot capture tree distance.
To integrate phylogenetic divergence directly into sequence attention, we generalize rotary embeddings from one-dimensional sequence indices to continuous metric coordinates \cite{su2024roformer}.
For each sequence $i$, its $d$-dimensional Buneman coordinate $\mathbf{x}_i \in \mathbb{R}^d$ parameterizes a set of rotary phase angles:
\begin{equation}
\theta_{i, m} = \sum_{c=1}^d x_{i, c} \Theta_{m, c},
\label{eq:tree_rope_angle}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{\Theta} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{head}}/2 \times d}$ is a fixed frequency projection matrix.
At each aligned site $u$, the sequence embedding generates query and key vectors $\mathbf{q}_i(u), \mathbf{k}_i(u) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{head}}}$.
We apply two-dimensional block-diagonal Givens rotation matrices $\mathbf{R}(\theta_i)$ to rotate query and key representations according to each taxon's continuous metric position.
Because the inner product satisfies $\langle \mathbf{R}(\theta_i) \mathbf{q}_i, \mathbf{R}(\theta_j) \mathbf{k}_j \rangle = \mathbf{q}_i^T \mathbf{R}(\theta_j - \theta_i) \mathbf{k}_j$, pairwise attention naturally attenuates as evolutionary divergence in Buneman metric space increases.
Rotations preserve geometry.

Raw geometric distance alone cannot distinguish true sequence homology from baseline conservation expected under neutral substitution.
Homology requires evolutionary calibration.
We therefore incorporate a log-space continuous-time Markov substitution prior directly into the attention logits.
For any sequence pair $(i, j)$ separated by evolutionary distance $d_{i, j}$, the transition probability under a neutral continuous-time Markov substitution model with equilibrium frequency $\epsilon_0$ is:
\begin{equation}
P_{i, j} = \epsilon_0 + (1 - \epsilon_0) \exp(-\lambda_h d_{i, j}),
\label{eq:phylo_prior}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_h$ is the global substitution scaling rate and $\epsilon_0 = 0.25$ for nucleotides.
We formulate the phylogenetic prior bias as $\text{PhyloBias}_{i, j} = \ln[\max(10^{-5}, P_{i, j})]$.
The composite unnormalized attention score between sequence $i$ and candidate reference $j$ at coordinate $u$ becomes:
\begin{equation}
S_u(i, j) = \frac{(\mathbf{R}(\theta_i) \mathbf{q}_i(u))^T (\mathbf{R}(\theta_j) \mathbf{k}_j(u))}{\sqrt{d_{\text{head}}}} + \text{PhyloBias}_{i, j}.
\label{eq:composite_attention}
\end{equation}
Unifying geometric distance with Markov substitution priors establishes a principled directed attention tensor along the chromosome.

\subsection{The Unpolarized Ancestral Root Sink Token}
\label{subsec:root_sink}

Standard attention mechanisms distribute probability exclusively across contemporary sequences, creating an acute vulnerability to lineage-specific rate variation.
Private mutations lack donors.
When an isolated lineage undergoes an episodic substitution burst---such as host-mediated APOBEC3G cytidine deamination or accelerated heterotachy---conventional models mistake localized divergent clusters for horizontal genetic exchange \cite{posada2001evaluation}.
Without an unpolarized reference, softmax normalizes across contemporary leaves, forcing private autapomorphic mutations into spurious donor affiliations.
False positives surge.
To insulate trajectory tracking from unreticulated rate bursts, \RhizAeon reserves index zero in the sequence dimension for an unpolarized ancestral root token $\mathbf{x}_{\text{root}}$.

The root token is anchored at the origin of the Buneman metric space:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{x}_{\text{root}} = \mathbf{0} \in \mathbb{R}^d.
\label{eq:root_anchor}
\end{equation}
For an alignment of $N$ contemporary sequences, the cross-attention operator operates over $N+1$ nodes, spanning the ancestral root and all contemporary leaves.
The softmax normalization strictly conserves probability across all $N+1$ nodes:
\begin{equation}
A_u(i \to \text{root}) + \sum_{j=1}^N A_u(i \to j) = 1.0.
\label{eq:root_softmax}
\end{equation}
Because the root token resides at the metric origin with neutral substitution priors, private substitutions that lack homologous support in other contemporary lineages naturally route their attention into node zero.
The root acts as an unpolarized sink.
Private noise is absorbed without perturbing leaf coordinates.
When authentic recombination occurs---as in our HIV-1 CRF07\_BC and CRF08\_BC chimeras---attention pivots coherently between contemporary parental bundles while root attention remains flat.
Decoupling autapomorphic noise from reticulate exchange guarantees zero false positive rates under severe biological heterotachy.
