\section{Discussion and Algorithmic Scaling}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Linear Manifold Geometry vs.\ Combinatorial Triplet Search}
\label{subsec:discussion_scaling}

Computational phylogenetic methods confront a difficult choice between evolutionary realism and combinatorial intractability.
Trees model divergence.
When reticulate events occur, forcing mosaic genomes into strictly bifurcating topologies distorts branch lengths and inflates substitution rate estimates \cite{schierup2000consequences}.
Conversely, modeling recombination explicitly through ancestral recombination graphs or phylogenetic changepoints introduces prohibitive computational scaling, demanding cubic or quartic algorithms that collapse on modern genomic datasets \cite{minh2020iqtree2}.
Triplet scanning methods like 3SEQ provide an empirical compromise by evaluating small three-sequence subgraphs \cite{boni2007exact}.
Triplet decomposition avoids tree estimation.
Yet exhaustive triplet testing scales cubically with sample size ($\binom{N}{3} \sim O(N^3)$), creating an inescapable computational barrier when surveillance cohorts expand to hundreds or thousands of isolates.

\RhizAeon demonstrates that continuous metric geometry dissolves this dichotomy.
By projecting whole-genome sequence divergence into fixed Buneman coordinates once for the entire alignment, the engine establishes an invariant spatial reference frame.
Zero Procrustes rotations are required.
Instantaneous sequence affinities are tracked continuously along the chromosome via analytical Gower out-of-sample projection, replacing discrete topology searches with smooth dynamical trajectories.
Manifolds preserve spatial continuity.
Cross-taxa attention operates against a fixed landmark basis ($K \le 32$), ensuring that trajectory extraction scales strictly as $O(N \cdot K \cdot L)$.
As our empirical benchmarks demonstrate, this linear scaling yields two- to three-order-of-magnitude accelerations over triplet tools on standard cohorts, finishing multi-kilobase scans in milliseconds where triplet enumeration requires minutes or hours.

Equally consequential is the resolution of complex multi-way mosaicism.
Triplet tools assume two-parent ancestry.
When circulating recombinant lineages accumulate segments from three or more parental lineages, examining triplets in isolation inevitably truncates mosaic tracts, missing breakpoints and hallucinating recombinant candidates.
By tracking continuous trajectory tensors across the entire cohort simultaneously, physical force field deconvolution decomposes multiple orthogonal spatial modes.
The framework detects interleaved parental contributions without prior constraints on parental cardinality.
Furthermore, metric isolation prevents unsampled ghost donors from triggering combinatorial false alarms across innocent contemporary clades.

\subsection{Data Governance and Biosafety Compliance}
\label{subsec:biosafety}

Research integrity demands strict transparency regarding computational protocols and data sources.
Computational analyses require ethical clarity.
We certify that this investigation represents a purely retrospective bioinformatic study operating exclusively on publicly accessible nucleotide sequences deposited in NCBI GenBank, GISAID, and the Los Alamos National Laboratory HIV Sequence Database.
No biological specimens, clinical samples, or viral cultures were collected, isolated, cultivated, or manipulated during this study.
All research was conducted entirely in silico using audited open-source algorithms.
The software performs automated sequence alignment scanning without generating actionable protocols for pathogen enhancement, gain-of-function phenotypes, or laboratory synthesis.
Source code, documentation, and client-side browser modules are freely available under permissive open-source licensing at \url{https://github.com/veg/rhizaeon-code}, promoting reproducible evolutionary genomics and accessible public health surveillance.
