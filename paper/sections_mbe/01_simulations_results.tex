\subsection{The Informational Horizon Across Simulated Whole Genomes}
\label{subsec:mbe_grand_sweep}

To systematically delineate the operational envelope of continuous manifold recombination detection without parameter tuning or cherry-picked test cases, we executed an exhaustive simulation sweep across 2,000 independent synthetic whole genomes ($1{,}000$ non-recombinant clonal nulls and $1{,}000$ recombinant chimeras; Table~\ref{tab:mbe_grand_sweep_envelope}, Figure~\ref{fig:mbe_grand_sweep_phase_diagram}).
The simulation grid systematically permuted five orthogonal evolutionary dimensions: genome length spanning compact viral genes to bacterial chromosomes ($L \in [1{,}000, 30{,}000]$~nt), cohort size ($N \in [4, 250]$~taxa), parental sequence divergence ($d_{\mathrm{parent}} \in [0.005, 0.25]$~subs/site), recombinant tract length ratio ($L_{\mathrm{tract}} / L \in [0.008, 0.50]$), and continuous Gamma site-rate heterogeneity ($\alpha \in [0.05, 5.0]$) alongside strict molecular clock controls ($\alpha \ge 50$).

Across all 1,000 non-recombinant null alignments, \RhizAeon maintained strict false positive rate control, triggering zero false alarms (alignment-level $\text{FPR} = 0.000\%$; $100.0\%$ specificity; Table~\ref{tab:mbe_grand_sweep_envelope}A, Figure~\ref{fig:mbe_grand_sweep_phase_diagram}B).
On strict molecular clock controls ($\alpha \ge 50$), the false alarm rate was $0.000\%$ (0 / 235).
Under severe biological heterotachy ($\alpha < 1.0$), rate variation yielded zero false alarms (0 / 501).
At the individual taxon level, across 66,542 non-recombinant leaves evaluated across the null corpus, zero leaves were falsely flagged as recombinant ($\text{FPR}_{\text{taxon}} = 0.0000\%$; $100.0\%$ per-taxon specificity).
Routing autapomorphic rate bursts through the unpolarized ancestral root token prevents localized mutational accelerations from mimicking horizontal donor introgression.

On the recombinant partition ($N_{\mathrm{rec}} = 1{,}000$), \RhizAeon's empirical power maps a clear physical phase transition governed by the Informative Mutation Horizon $M_{\mathrm{tract}} = d_{\mathrm{parent}} \cdot L_{\mathrm{tract}}$ (Table~\ref{tab:mbe_grand_sweep_envelope}B, Figure~\ref{fig:mbe_grand_sweep_phase_diagram}A).
When an imported cassette contains fewer than three informative mutations ($M_{\mathrm{tract}} \le 2$), detection power drops to $0.46\%$ (1 / 219), reflecting the fundamental information-theoretic limit where phylogenetic incongruence cannot be distinguished from random neutral substitutions along a clonal branch.
As informative mutational density increases, detection escalates sharply: power reaches $24.68\%$ at $M_{\mathrm{tract}} \in [3, 5]$ SNPs, $56.20\%$ at $M_{\mathrm{tract}} \in [6, 10]$ SNPs, passes $78.31\%$ at $M_{\mathrm{tract}} \in [11, 15]$ SNPs, and reaches $96.64\%$ for $M_{\mathrm{tract}} > 100$ SNPs (115 / 119 trials).
Spatial accuracy mirrors this informative transition (Figure~\ref{fig:mbe_grand_sweep_phase_diagram}C): when detected, the median breakpoint error decreases from 19.0~nt at $M_{\mathrm{tract}} \in [6, 10]$ to 11.0~nt for $M_{\mathrm{tract}} > 100$, while mean tract overlap achieves an informative Jaccard index of $0.955$.
Across the entire 2,000-trial suite, \RhizAeon sustained a median runtime of 556.7~ms per whole genome.

Two operational dynamics govern the saturation envelope and explain alignment-level versus isolate-level sensitivity.
First, in observable regimes, \RhizAeon reaches $100.0\%$ alignment-level recall.
In small cohorts ($N \in [4, 8]$) with sufficient mutational information ($M_{\mathrm{tract}} \ge 50$), \RhizAeon achieved $100.00\%$ alignment-level recall, $100.00\%$ taxon recall, and $100.00\%$ precision (21 / 21 trials).
Across deep divergence regimes ($M_{\mathrm{tract}} \ge 500$), alignment-level recall was likewise $100.00\%$ (13 / 13 in the sweep; 63 / 63 in targeted stress trials).
The residual 4 non-detections at $M_{\mathrm{tract}} > 100$ (3.36\%) occurred exclusively under adversarial edge cases: severe Gamma rate clustering ($\alpha < 0.27$, where substitutions cluster into narrow hyper-mutable spikes that pool into the unpolarized root token to prevent false alarms), or ultra-diffuse parental divergence ($d_{\mathrm{parent}} \le 0.011$ across $>10$~kb spans).
Across high-information cohorts with moderate divergence ($M_{\mathrm{tract}} \ge 50, d_{\mathrm{parent}} \ge 0.02$), alignment recall reached $91.67\%$ (187 / 204) with $95.07\%$ precision.

Second, the difference between alignment-level power ($96.64\%$) and taxon-level recall ($89.98\%$) at $M_{\mathrm{tract}} > 100$ arises from the demographic structure of descendant swarms ($k_{\mathrm{true}} \le 0.35 N$ isolates).
In balanced trees where the imported tract spans near half the chromosome ($L_{\mathrm{tract}} / L \approx 0.40\text{--}0.50$), \RhizAeon detects the physical reticulation with near-perfect spatial fidelity (mean Jaccard $J = 0.955$, median breakpoint error $11.0$~nt) but exhibits unrooted polarity transposition, attributing the mosaicism to the background sister while designating the recombinant swarm as donor.
Because automated scoring strictly evaluates isolates carrying the \texttt{Rec\_} prefix, unrooted parental transpositions receive zero taxon-recall credit despite accurate physical event discovery.
Conditioned on parental polarity orientation, mean taxon recall was $98.2\%$, with over $93\%$ of detected trials capturing every descendant isolate in the swarm.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textit{Bacterial Micro-Conversions (900 trials, $L_{\mathrm{tract}} \in [50, 800]$~nt)}:
    When a 50~nt conversion tract imports into a 5,000~nt chromosome ($1.0\%$ of genome length) at $d_{\mathrm{parent}} = 0.02$, the tract carries on average only 0.8 distinguishing mutations. Both \RhizAeon and 3SEQ declare zero events ($\hat{K} = 0.00$, $\text{FPR} = 0.0\%$), respecting the physical Nyquist floor. As tract length expands ($L_{\mathrm{tract}} \ge 100$~nt, $d_{\mathrm{parent}} \ge 0.05$), \RhizAeon captures micro-tracts with $84.5\%$--$100.0\%$ alignment recall, achieving $99.98\%$ overall precision with an un-introgressed background parental false positive rate of $\text{FPR}_{\mathrm{parent}} = 0.0037\%$ (Table~\ref{tab:mbe_controlled_benchmarks}C). In contrast, isolated triplet tests begin misidentifying un-introgressed background leaves as recombinant children: at $L_{\mathrm{tract}} = 800$~nt and $d_{\mathrm{parent}} = 0.10$, 3SEQ's parental false alarm rate surges to $99.8\%$ ($39.95$ of $40$ taxa falsely accused), emitting up to 40 uncollapsed events per alignment and deflating precision to $8.2\%$.
    
    \item \textit{Unsampled Ghost Reservoirs (540 trials)}:
    In natural zoonoses and bacterial transformation, donor lineages are frequently unsampled. In the absence of contemporary donor sequences, 3SEQ systematically pairs the recombinant child with an un-introgressed background leaf, suffering a $100.0\%$ parental false alarm rate ($\text{FPR}_{\mathrm{parent}} = 100.0\%$) and emitting a mean of 23.7 candidate events per single introgression. By anchoring metric coordinates with an unrooted origin channel, \RhizAeon isolates off-manifold trajectories lacking an opposing contemporary leaf dipole, achieving $100.0\%$ alignment-level recall, attributing the introgression to \texttt{Ghost} with $99.4\%$ donor accuracy, and maintaining $0.00\%$ parental false alarms across all 540 trials with mean Jaccard overlap of $J = 0.946$ (Table~\ref{tab:mbe_controlled_benchmarks}B).
    
    \item \textit{Epidemic Swarms and Multi-Way Cassettes (540 trials)}:
    When an ancestral recombination event expands into an epidemic swarm ($K = 2, 5, 10$ descendant isolates with ongoing transmission drift), pairwise counting treats each isolate as an independent reticulation, inflating event counts up to 38-fold ($\hat{K} = 38.2$). \RhizAeon achieves $97.0\%$--$100.0\%$ alignment recall and groups co-recombinant sisters into an average of $\hat{K} = 0.97$ unique ancestral events with $96.3\%$ taxon recall (Table~\ref{tab:mbe_controlled_benchmarks}A). On complex multi-parental architectures (dual cassettes, crossover cascades, and nested Russian dolls), \RhizAeon resolves distinct flanking and internal donors ($J = 0.865$) while suppressing parental false alarms to $0.62\%$.
\end{enumerate}
